Hold up a hand.
Make a turkey.

GobbleVision uses an in-browser hand-pose model to turn your pose into a one-of-a-kind digital turkey. Decorate it, download it, and send it to somebody you are thankful for.

● Your camera stays on your device. No photo or video is uploaded.

How it works

A familiar craft, a new kind of tracing paper.

01

Find your hand

The model estimates 21 landmarks from the live camera feed. Processing happens in your browser.

02

Build the bird

GobbleVision maps your pose into a playful turkey made from a body, feathers, beak, feet, and attitude.

03

Finish the story

Add your own marks, download the image, and share it. Your artwork remains yours.

Made for Thanksgiving by the Design Research Collective at Carnegie Mellon University.
